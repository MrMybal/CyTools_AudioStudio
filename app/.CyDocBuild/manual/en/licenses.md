## Application and libraries {#application}

Read the application’s `LICENSE` and the component notices in `LICENCES`. Options lists component licences and source links. The licence of CyDoc or CyToolsCore does not replace the application’s licence.

## Models and external engines {#models}

Downloaded weights, external engines and providers retain their own terms. Some model variants allow only non-commercial use. Check the exact model and revision selected for your work; a different variant can have different terms.
